# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Internal staff at a company using the portal to run recruitment: **Admins** (full control, user/role/permission management), **HR** (manage job positions, candidates, evaluation forms, offers), and **Interviewers** (review assigned candidates, submit feedback and evaluations). Used at a desk, during or right after interviews, often between other tasks — needs to be fast and low-friction, not a destination product.

## Product Purpose
A recruitment pipeline tool: track job positions, candidates, interviews, feedback, and structured evaluation forms (status, expected/offered CTC, joining date) through to hire. Success looks like an HR/Interviewer completing their task (logging feedback, moving a candidate's status, scheduling an interview) in as few clicks as possible, with role/permission boundaries that stay invisible until they matter.

## Brand Personality
Efficient, trustworthy, clean. Feels like well-built internal tooling — competent and fast, not flashy. Confidence comes from clarity (clear states, clear hierarchy) rather than decoration.

## Anti-references
- **Bloated enterprise SaaS**: no dense nested menus, no Salesforce/SAP-style information overload, no over-modaled workflows.
- **Generic AI-template look**: no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no cream/tan/sand default backgrounds, no identical icon-card grids, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows scaffolding every section.
- Keep the existing indigo-on-dark-sidebar identity; don't drift toward generic "modern SaaS purple" cliché tropes beyond what's already committed.

## Design Principles
- Clarity over decoration — every visual choice should reduce time-to-task, not add polish for its own sake.
- Status is always legible at a glance (badges, table states) — color + label, never color alone.
- Permission boundaries are invisible by default: hidden nav/actions, not disabled-and-explained.
- Density with breathing room: data-dense tables and forms stay scannable, not cramped.
- Consistency beats novelty: reuse the existing card/table/badge/button system rather than inventing new patterns per screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA: sufficient contrast (body text ≥4.5:1), full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, no color-only status indicators, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
